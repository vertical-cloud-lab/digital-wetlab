### 2. Adequacy of the Experimental Design

However, a critical limitation is that binary precipitation scoring (precipitate observed vs. not observed) provides far less information per data point than continuous measurements of dissolved metal concentrations or osmotic/activity coefficients. Each binary observation constrains only whether the saturation index (SI) is above or below zero at that composition; it does not provide the magnitude of supersaturation or the individual ion activities needed to decompose composite activity-coefficient products into individual binary parameters.
