Question: Have Pitzer mixing parameters theta(Pb2+, Fe2+) or psi(Pb2+, Fe2+, anion) ever been determined, and if so from what systems?

**I could not verify a published determination of either** \(\theta(\mathrm{Pb^{2+},Fe^{2+}})\) **or** \(\psi(\mathrm{Pb^{2+},Fe^{2+},anion})\). In particular, the reviewed Pitzer parameter compilation for both metals lists no Pb–Fe mixing term. This is a *not-found* conclusion, rather than proof that no such fit exists anywhere; consequently, I cannot identify a Pb–Fe system from which either requested parameter was determined. (turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 3-6, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 6-9, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 9-13, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 13-17)

The distinction from parameters that **have** been determined is important:

For chloride, Moog, Hagemann, and Rumyantsev’s 2004 isopiestic work determined Fe(II) mixing parameters with **Na⁺, K⁺, Mg²⁺, and Ca²⁺**, using FeCl₂ mixed with each corresponding chloride—not with PbCl₂. For sulfate, a 2025 study fitted \(\theta(\mathrm{NH_4^+,Fe^{2+}})=-0.05\) and \(\psi(\mathrm{NH_4^+,Fe^{2+},SO_4^{2-}})=0.0050\) from the **(NH₄)₂SO₄–FeSO₄–H₂O** system; its authors note that the Fe-system solubility data are sparse and scattered. Neither study supplies a Pb–Fe coefficient. (turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 6-9, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 30-35, talrejamuthreja2025thermodynamicsof(nh4)2so4(aq) pages 14-17)

Finally, separately reported Pb–Cl and Fe–Cl/Fe–SO₄ parameters do not determine a *cross-cation* \(\theta\) or \(\psi\). For Pb–sulfate, the reviewed compilation reports a PbSO₄⁰ formation constant rather than Pitzer coefficients, citing the low solubility of PbSO₄. Thus those individual-metal systems cannot be offered as the provenance of a fitted Pb²⁺–Fe²⁺ interaction. (turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 3-6, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 9-13, turner2023sourcesofpitzer pages 13-17)
