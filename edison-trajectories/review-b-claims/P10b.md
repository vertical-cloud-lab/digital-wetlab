Question: Up to what molality can the Pitzer model reproduce osmotic and activity coefficients of aqueous NaNO3 at 25 C, and is a Cphi term needed? What parameters did Marion (2002) use for NaNO3?

At **25 °C**, an independently documented Pitzer fit by **May et al. (2011)** treats aqueous NaNO₃ osmotic and mean activity coefficients through **10.75 mol kg⁻¹** (reported as **10.8 mol kg⁻¹** when rounded in their main table). Its fitted concentration-independent third-virial parameter is **C = −5.18 × 10⁻⁵**: very small, but **not zero**. The retrieved evidence therefore does not establish that a *Cφ* term can be omitted without degrading or refitting the model. (may2011agenericand pages 9-12, may2011agenericand pages 30-33, may2011agenericand pages 41-43)

The parameter sets must not be conflated:
