---

Question: Has the Pitzer mixing parameter theta(Na+, Pb2+) been determined from data for the NaNO3-Pb(NO3)2-H2O system (solubility or isopiestic), and how does it compare with values derived from Na-Pb-Cl systems?

**No nitrate-system determination was verified.** Solubility measurements exist for NaNO₃–Pb(NO₃)₂–H₂O, notably those of Glasstone and Saunders (1923), but the located sources do not report a Pitzer fit of those measurements to the Na⁺–Pb²⁺ cation-mixing parameter, θ(Na⁺,Pb²⁺). Arrad’s 2019 Pitzer study instead fits *binary* Pb(NO₃)₂–H₂O osmotic-coefficient and vapor-pressure data. Its reported β⁽⁰⁾, β⁽¹⁾ and Cφ coefficients cannot determine an interaction between Na⁺ and Pb²⁺, because Na⁺ is absent from the fitted solutions. I likewise found no verified θ fitted to isopiestic measurements of the nitrate ternary. This is a limit of the located literature, not proof that such a fit has never been made. (arrad2019thermodynamicmodelingof pages 1-2, arrad2019thermodynamicmodelingof pages 3-5, arrad2019thermodynamicmodelingof pages 6-7, arrad2019thermodynamicmodelingof pages 2-2)

The **documented comparison value is θ(Na⁺,Pb²⁺) = +0.10 at 25 °C** in a Na–Pb chloride-containing brine model. Xiong and colleagues tabulate it as a *literature parameter from Felmy et al. (2000)*, rather than a value newly fitted to nitrate data. The available secondary table establishes its value and attribution, but not which measurements Felmy and colleagues used to obtain it. Consequently, there is **no independently measured nitrate θ to compare numerically with +0.10**; using +0.10 for the nitrate ternary would be an unvalidated transfer from the chloride-based model, not a nitrate-system determination. (xiong2017apitzermodel pages 1-13, xiong2017apitzermodel pages 13-19, xiong2017apitzermodel media 279ce06f)
