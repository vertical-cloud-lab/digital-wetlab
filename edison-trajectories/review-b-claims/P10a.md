Question: What Pitzer mixing parameters theta(Na,Ca) and psi(Na,Ca,Cl) were determined by Holmes, Baes and Mesmer (1981) and by Oakes, Pitzer and Sterner (1998), from what measurements and over what temperature range?

The two studies constrained Na⁺–Ca²⁺–Cl⁻ mixing interactions using different observables: **isopiestic water-activity data** in 1981 and **calorimetric enthalpy data** in 1998. The accessible evidence establishes one temperature-dependent θ expression, but not a reliable numerical ψ value or the 1998 fitted coefficients. (mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 277-280, mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 280-282, 曾贻善2002高温高压下电解质溶液热力学性质的研究进展a pages 1-3)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Holmes, Baes & Mesmer (1981) | Isopiestic osmotic-coefficient measurements on aqueous NaCl–CaCl₂ mixtures, 25–201 °C (298–474 K). (oakes1992phaseequilibriaand pages 19-21, mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 277-280) | $\theta_{\mathrm{Na,Ca}}=10.7/T-0.0316$, with $T$ in K; approximately $0.0043$ at 25 °C to $-0.0090$ at 201 °C. (mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 277-280) | No defensible numerical value was confirmed. For Holmes, Baes and Mesmer, the reported expression is **θ(Na,Ca) = 10.7/T − 0.0316**, with *T* in kelvin, over **25–201 °C**. Their isopiestic osmotic coefficients were reproducible to within 0.5% even without mixing terms. Fitting θ approximately halved the standard deviation of isothermal fits; fitting ψ(Na,Ca,Cl) as well gave no significant further improvement. Thus the available account does **not** establish a useful independent numerical ψ determination. (mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 277-280, mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 280-282)

Oakes, Pitzer and Sterner measured **heats of dilution and mixing** of aqueous NaCl–CaCl₂ with a flow-through calorimeter at **373–573 K (100–300 °C) and 21.5 MPa**. Those enthalpy measurements probe the temperature dependence of the Na–Ca and Na–Ca–Cl interactions, but the accessible sources do not reproduce their numerical θ and ψ fits. Assigning specific 1998 coefficients—or treating enthalpy measurements alone as direct determinations of absolute θ and ψ—would therefore go beyond the evidence retrieved here. (曾贻善2002高温高压下电解质溶液热力学性质的研究进展a pages 1-3)

**Numerical caution:** a second passage in the secondary report gives a different range for Holmes’s θ values. The endpoint values above are calculated from its explicitly stated equation, rather than taken from that inconsistent range. (mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 277-280, mariner2004indriftprecipitatessaltsmodel pages 280-282)
