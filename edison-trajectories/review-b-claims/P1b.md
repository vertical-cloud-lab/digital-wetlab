Question: What total dissolved Fe and Pb concentrations have been measured in equilibrium with mackinawite FeS and with galena PbS between pH 4 and 9 at 25 C, in water and in NaCl solutions? Are they millimolal or lower?

## Measured concentrations and limits of the evidence

The retrieved literature does **not** establish a measured range of *total dissolved* Fe and Pb for both minerals throughout pH 4–9 at 25 °C in both water and NaCl solutions. The directly comparable numbers are more limited:

| Solid and medium | Reported dissolved concentration | What it establishes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Galena (PbS), water, 25 °C** | **0.94 × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹ Pb** (approximately **0.94 µmol kg⁻¹**) | A reported experimental PbS solubility, roughly **0.00094 millimolal**. The cited measurement does not provide a pH 4–9 series. (clever1980thesolubilityof pages 20-21) |
| **Mackinawite (FeS), aqueous equilibrium** | Maximum **10⁻⁵·⁸⁸ mol L⁻¹**, approximately **1.3 µM**, for *FeS(aq) expressed as a monomer* | **Not total dissolved Fe.** Other dissolved iron species contribute, particularly as pH changes; this number cannot serve as an upper bound for total Fe. (rickard2007chemistryofiron pages 18-20, rickard2007chemistryofiron pages 17-18) |
| **Galena, 3.0 molal NaCl–HCl solution** | Experiments reported at **310.15–473.15 K** and pH **2–5** | These are **not 25 °C measurements** and do not cover pH 4–9. They cannot supply a 25 °C saline Pb concentration. (clever1980thesolubilityof pages 20-21) |

**Are they millimolal or lower?** The verified **25 °C water measurement for Pb is far below millimolal**. The approximately 1.3 µM FeS(aq) figure is also submillimolal, **but it is one modeled dissolved species, not measured total Fe**. On the evidence retrieved, it would be unjustified to say that *all total dissolved Fe and Pb concentrations* in equilibrium with these solids are below 1 mmol kg⁻¹ across pH 4–9 and NaCl concentrations. Such a conclusion requires the corresponding total-metal measurements and specified solution conditions, which were not available here. (clever1980thesolubilityof pages 20-21, rickard2007chemistryofiron pages 18-20, rickard2007chemistryofiron pages 17-18)
