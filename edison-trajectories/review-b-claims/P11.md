Question: What activity-coefficient models does the OLI Systems software use (its aqueous model and the mixed-solvent electrolyte MSE model), are they based on the Pitzer equations, and are their parameter databanks published or proprietary?

OLI distinguishes its **aqueous (AQ) Bromley–Zemaitis model** from its **mixed-solvent electrolyte (MSE) model**. The AQ activity-coefficient treatment extends Debye–Hückel with Bromley-type ion-interaction terms and additional temperature-dependent terms; it is **not simply the conventional Pitzer electrolyte-virial equation**. One published description also reports supplementary Pitzer treatment for ion–molecule and molecule–molecule interactions in OLI’s aqueous implementation, so “OLI never uses Pitzer” would be too categorical. (lin2009comparisonofactivity pages 3-5, lacour2005comparisonofsimulators pages 1-2)

MSE instead combines a **Pitzer–Debye–Hückel long-range electrostatic term**, a **UNIQUAC short-range term**, and a separate, ionic-strength-dependent **second-virial-type interaction term** for specific ion–ion and ion–molecule interactions. The word *Pitzer* in “Pitzer–Debye–Hückel” identifies one component; it does **not** mean that MSE as a whole is the conventional Pitzer ion-interaction model. Historically, an earlier OLI program, ECES, also offered **Bromley or Pitzer** as alternative activity-coefficient options. (lin2009comparisonofactivity pages 2-3, sinquefield1991amicrocomputersoftware pages 9-14)

**Parameters are a separate issue from published equations.** The historical ECES databank was explicitly described as an “extensive private database.” The sources examined do not establish that OLI has published either *complete current* AQ or MSE built-in parameter databank. They do explicitly state that **some MSE parameters have been published** and that users can determine or modify parameters with OLI’s Regress program. Thus, the defensible distinction is **commercial/private comprehensive databanks, not a claim that every individual parameter is secret**. (sinquefield1991amicrocomputersoftware pages 9-14, lin2009comparisonofactivity pages 3-5)
