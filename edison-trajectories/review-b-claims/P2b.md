## Aqueous acetate-complex stability

Let Ac⁻ denote CH₃COO⁻. The constants below are **cumulative** formation constants: β₁ = a(MAc⁺)/[a(M²⁺)a(Ac⁻)] and β₂ = a(M(Ac)₂)/[a(M²⁺)a(Ac⁻)²]. Thus, log β₂ is **not** the log constant for adding a second acetate to MAc⁺. Giordano explicitly defines the Pb constants in terms of activities and reports values extrapolated to infinite dilution. (giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 4-5, giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 1-2)

The following comparison separates Giordano’s experimentally derived Pb(II) values, the published compilation of Palmer–Drummond Fe(II) values, and later *extrapolations* for Pb(II). (giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 1-2, giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility media 8668a003, giordano1994organicliganddistribution pages 16-17, giordano1994organicliganddistribution pages 14-16)

**Temperature dependence.** Both complexes become more stable on heating in these reported series. For Pb(II), log β₁ increases from **2.4 ± 0.1 at 25 °C** to **2.7 ± 0.2 at 85 °C**, while log β₂ increases from **3.4 ± 0.4** to **4.3 ± 0.2**. For Fe(II), the compiled log β₁/log β₂ pair increases from **0.87/1.89 at 25 °C** to **2.40/4.00 at 200 °C**; a separate 300 °C tabulation gives **3.70/6.82**. The compiled Pb values at 100–200 °C are explicitly **extrapolated from 25–85 °C data**, not measurements at those temperatures. (giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 1-2, giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility media 8668a003, giordano1994organicliganddistribution pages 16-17, giordano1994organicliganddistribution pages 14-16)

**NaCl-media qualification.** Giordano measured anglesite solubility in equal-molal *acetic acid–sodium acetate* solutions (each component 0.001–0.25 molal), not in a specified NaCl background. NaCl parameters entered the **activity-coefficient calculation**; their appearance in the method does not turn the reported infinite-dilution Pb constants into measured NaCl-medium constants. The study selected runs at ionic strength ≤0.1 for deriving those values. (giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 1-2, giordano1989anglesite(pbso4)solubility pages 4-5)

For Fe(II), a later study reproduces **temperature- and ionic-strength-dependent expressions** for the concentration quotients Q₁ = [FeAc⁺]/([Fe²⁺][Ac⁻]) and Q₂ = [Fe(Ac)₂]/([Fe²⁺][Ac⁻]²), attributing them to earlier potentiometric work. It applies them at **80 °C in 1 wt% NaCl**, calculating **11.23% FeAc⁺** and **2.47% Fe(Ac)₂** under its specified solution conditions. Those percentages are modeled speciation, **not measured NaCl-specific log β values**. A Palmer–Hyde study of ferrous chloride and acetate complexation to 300 °C was identified, but its primary text could not be checked here; consequently, no numerical NaCl-medium stability constant from that study is asserted. (ayyagari2023effectofacetic pages 1-3, ayyagari2023effectofacetic pages 3-5)
