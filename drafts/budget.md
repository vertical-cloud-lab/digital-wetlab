# Budget Justification (Copy-Paste for PRF Portal)

> **Prompt:** "Describe the intended use of grant funds including number and level of students receiving support. If field work is budgeted, describe the purpose and location, and the number of students involved. Additionally, specify how those funds will be allocated..."

---

Grant funds ($110,000 over 32 months, January 1, 2027 – August 31, 2029) will primarily support student research training. Three students will receive support: one M.S.-level graduate student and two undergraduate researchers.

Student Support ($81,953; 74.5% of total). One master's-level graduate student will receive a stipend of $29,989/year ($59,978 total) to conduct FeS and PbS precipitation experiments in the presence of petroleum-derived compounds, PHREEQC thermodynamic modeling, and Pitzer parameter regression. The student will gain training in aqueous geochemistry, high-throughput experimentation using an Opentrons OT-2 robotic liquid handler (Python API), PHREEQC thermodynamic modeling, and nonlinear parameter regression. Graduate tuition support totaling $15,975 covers 30 credits over two academic years at BYU's per-credit rate. Two undergraduate students will be supported at $6,000 total for part-time research during the academic year and/or summer, assisting with stock solution preparation, image analysis, data entry, and quality-control checks.

PI Summer Stipend ($16,000). The PI requests 0.5 months of summer salary per year at $8,000/year including fringe benefits. This time will be dedicated to mentoring students, analyzing data, and preparing manuscripts. The $8,000/year cap including fringe is the maximum allowed by ACS PRF.

Conference Travel ($4,000). Travel funds will support the PI and students presenting research results at national conferences such as the ACS National Meeting. Budget covers registration, transportation, and lodging.

Supplies and Materials ($8,047). Funds will support consumable chemicals (FeSO4, FeCl2, PbCl2, Na2S·9H2O, NaHS, NaCl, CaCl2, sodium acetate, sodium propionate, cyclohexane carboxylic acid, pH buffer reagents, ICP-OES standards, ultrapure water) and disposable labware (chemically resistant polypropylene and optically clear polystyrene 96-well microplates, adhesive plate seals, filter-tipped pipette tips for the OT-2). Estimated breakdown: consumable chemicals and reagents ($2,500), 96-well microplates ($2,000), filter-tipped pipette tips ($2,500), and adhesive plate seals, gloves, and miscellaneous consumables ($1,047). Supplies and experimental costs beyond this will be supplemented by the PI's startup equipment and supplies budget, which includes the service fees for ICP-OES usage.

No field work is budgeted. No funds are requested for overhead/indirect costs, capital equipment, publication costs, laboratory technicians, contractors, consultants, or visiting faculty, in compliance with ACS PRF policy.
